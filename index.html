the 7th, again

Nicole,

Two years and five months ago, on the 7th of February, I had no idea I was about to get lucky for a very long time.

Twenty-nine months later, and the 7th still means something. It has quietly become a little checkpoint — a day I get to stop and name, on purpose, everything about loving you that I usually just live inside of without saying out loud.

Even though how many times I forgot our monthsaries, you are always the first one to rememeber it, I don't know if you realize it, but you make me feel like the luckiest person alive. You make me feel like I can be my best self, and that I can be loved for it. You make me feel like I can be my worst self, and that I can still be loved for it.

You have this way of making ordinary days feel worth remembering even though I don't remember them the next day HAHAHA. You bring up things I said in passing, weeks after I forgot I said them. You show up, even on the days I make it hard to.

I don't have a big gesture for you in our today's monthsarry. Just this — a small page with your name on it, and an attempt to write down twenty-nine reasons out of the much longer list I actually carry around.

Happy 29 months, Baba. Here's to the 7th, and to every ordinary day around it, I love you.

— yours
